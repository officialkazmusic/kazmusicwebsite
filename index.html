ABOUT KAZ

Founded in 2020 and hailing from Baguio City, KAZ is an OPM hip-hop duo composed of Zy and unknown¿. Having sharpened their skills and shared musical influences together back in high school, the duo operates at the intersection of traditional boom-bap grit and refined modern production, treating hip-hop as a deliberate medium for structural commentary. Their sound bridges the raw, unpolished kinetic energy of early hip-hop with contemporary sonic sensibilities, resulting in an approach that feels grounded yet forward-facing.

At the core of KAZ's identity is an uncompromising commitment to sharp, concept-driven penmanship. Abandoning surface-level tropes and empty posturing, their discography centers on honest, blunt, and thought-provoking themes designed to challenge listener perspective. Every bar serves a distinct conceptual purpose—delivering unfiltered commentary through structured, high-caliber lyricism that demands active engagement rather than passive consumption.

Zy brings raw, kinetic energy and a sharp, emphatic delivery to KAZ. Balancing a confident, assertive presence with a grounded, straightforward approach to writing, his strength lies in converting simple, unfiltered truths into impactful, resonant lines. Rather than overcomplicating his penmanship with dense technicality, Zy utilizes a unique, accessible approach to metaphors that ensures every bar lands cleanly, serving as the group's direct, front-line force.

Functioning as the sonic architect of KAZ, unknown¿ shapes the group's musical palette while bringing a laid-back, calculated presence to the mic. As the primary beatmaker and producer, he crafts the atmospheric blend of gritty, old-school boom-bap and modern production that defines their sound. Lyrically, unknown¿ operates with a nuanced touch—layering his messages deep within metaphors and subtle subtext, providing a refined, contemplative counterweight to the duo's vocal dynamic.


Together, Zy's explosive delivery and unknown¿'s intricate production create a balanced dynamic that defines KAZ. Rooted in their shared Baguio roots and high school upbringing, the OPM duo combines blunt realism with layered conceptual depth, bridging classic hip-hop roots and contemporary taste to deliver thought-provoking, uncompromised music.
