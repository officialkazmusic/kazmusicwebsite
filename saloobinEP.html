SALOOBIN EP

Release Date: May 20,2022

Listen on Spotify:

Serving as the debut project from KAZ, Saloobin EP stands as an unvarnished introduction to the Baguio-bred duo. True to its title, the project acts as a direct outlet for the group's deep insights, opinions, and unfiltered perspectives on the world around them. While rooted in a more raw, foundational sound compared to their later technical evolution, the EP showcases their signature straightforward penmanship and blunt lyrical delivery. It marks the precise moment Zy and unknown¿ made their presence heard, laying down a strong, uncompromising foundation that signaled the group's immense potential from day one.

TRACK LIST :

1. Intro

Serving as the opening statement of the Saloobin EP, "Intro" sets the tone for KAZ's arrival over a gritty, classic boom-bap production. The track captures the duo navigating the tension between ambitious drive and raw reality, laying bare their aspirations, artistic goals, and the internal and external doubts that accompany their push to make an impact in the rap industry. Driven by a sense of urgency and early enthusiasm, Zy and unknown¿ deliver an unvarnished declaration of intent—establishing their mindset, hunger, and determination from the very first beat. The track concludes with a historical audio snippet of Johnny Antillon's 1950s speech—a reflection on favoring the active strivings, plans, and fortunate reality of the present century over an idle utopia—functioning as a thematic bridge that seamlessly carries the project into the next track, "Ibabaw."

2. Ibabaw

Opening with a soulful vocal sample before sliding into a smooth, groovy rhythm, "Ibabaw" pairs a chill, relaxed groove with an underlying undertone of urban anxiety. The track addresses the modern human condition—critiquing society's tendency to rush through life, fixate on material pursuits, and fail to pause and present themselves in the moment. Central to the song is its thought-provoking chorus, which directly challenges the listener with the question "Gaano kalalim ang kaya mong isipin?" ("How deep are you capable of thinking?"), before delivering its central thesis: "Silang takot malunod ang nasa mababaw" ("Those who are afraid to drown are the ones who stay in the shallows").
Here, KAZ executes a double entendre with the title Ibabaw: referencing both mababaw (the shallow waters or shores) and mundong ibabaw (the Earth's surface)—a metaphor for surface-level living and those who navigate life without depth. Anchored by catchy melodies, the chorus serves as an invitation to look past the groove and unpack Zy and unknown¿'s intricate verses, which are packed with multi-layered metaphors that highlight their growing lyrical dexterity.

3. Saloobin (Interlude)

Serving as a brief yet emotionally heavy interlude, "Saloobin" stands as a raw solo moment from unknown¿ and the project's shortest track. Set against an eerie, anxious instrumental, the beat creates an unsettling atmosphere that mirrors the verse's inner turmoil. Here, unknown¿ delivers an intensely honest look into his personal struggles—confronting crippling fear, deep-seated anxiety, and self-doubt. Yet, beneath the weight of these vulnerabilities, subtle glints of hope emerge, revealing the artist's deepest emotions and offering an unfiltered glimpse into his mental landscape.

4. Retokado

Serving as the EP's lead single and an intentional instrumental juxtapose to "Saloobin," "Retokado" shifts the project's atmosphere with a lively groove and an uplifting, energetic vibe. Beneath its vibrant rhythm, the track directly addresses the tension between authenticity and artificiality. unknown¿ anchors the song's core message on the hook, delivering a melodic and infectious chorus centered on choosing uncompromising honesty over fake acceptance. Taking full command of the verse, Zy brings his signature straightforward writing and high-octane delivery, weaponizing the "Retokado"—or cosmetic surgery—metaphor to critique those who alter or disguise their real identities to gain superficial approval.

5. Marites

Serving as the compelling final track of the Saloobin EP, "Marites" delivers a sharp social commentary targeted at local gossip culture and modern rumor-mongering. In a clever conceptual turn, the song both complements and counters the EP's overarching theme: while Saloobin champions expressing one's authentic inner voice, "Marites" argues that not every saloobin or opinion needs to be aired—especially when it serves only to tear others down. Zy and unknown¿ close out their debut project with a biting critique of mindless chatter, offering a mature, balanced conclusion that emphasizes intention, self-restraint, and meaningful speech over destructive gossip.
